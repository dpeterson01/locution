## Locution 0.1.52

Fixes for recording, shortcuts, and local cleanup setup, plus a narrower focus on macOS and Windows.

## What's new

- **Cleaner starts to back-to-back recordings** - audio from the previous recording no longer leaks into the first moments of the next one.
- **Escape is released after short recordings** - the cancel shortcut no longer stays captured system-wide after a very quick recording.
- **Mute while recording respects your mute** - if your system was already muted, Locution leaves it muted when recording ends.
- **Better filler removal** - removing a leading "um" keeps the sentence capitalized, and "Ha" is no longer treated as an English filler word.
- **No recording without a model** - Locution won't open the microphone when no transcription model is available to process it.
- **Local cleanup setup pulls the right models** - "Set up local cleanup" now downloads the Ollama models cleanup actually uses.
- **Steadier on quit and on Intel Macs** - fixed a crash on quit, and Intel Macs no longer try Metal acceleration, which could hang model loading.

Locution now ships for macOS and Windows only. Linux packages are no longer built.
