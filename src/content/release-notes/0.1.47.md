## Locution 0.1.47

On-device cleanup with Apple Foundation Models.

## What's new

- **Apple Foundation Models cleanup (macOS 26+)** — a new local Cleanup provider that uses the language model built into Apple Intelligence. No install and no separate server; nothing leaves your Mac. Choose "Apple Foundation Models" under Cleanup in Settings to try it.
- **A gentle hand-off for long dictations** — Apple's on-device model has a fixed context window. When a dictation runs past it, Locution keeps your exact transcript and points you to Ollama for cleanup on longer passages, rather than trimming anything.

## Requirements

- Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 26 (Tahoe) or newer, with Apple Intelligence enabled, for the new provider.
- Ollama stays available for cleanup on any Mac and for longer dictations.

Built on Handy. Thanks to the Handy project, Whisper, and Ollama.
