## Locution 0.1.49

Fixes Apple Foundation Models cleanup being skipped on new installs.

## What's new

- **Apple Foundation Models cleanup runs again on Apple Silicon** — new installs defaulted to Apple's on-device model without setting its model name, so cleanup quietly returned your raw transcript instead of cleaning it. It now works out of the box. If you use Ollama or a cloud provider, you were unaffected.
